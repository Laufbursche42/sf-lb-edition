Ride log
Record ride log
Off by default. Records the complete telemetry snapshot the scooter sends, once per minute while you ride: speeds, gear, power, currents, pack and pole voltage, motor temperatures, trip and total distance, status and error bits plus the whole battery block with every cell voltage. Recording starts as soon as the scooter first moves. All rides are kept until you delete them.
View the exported CSV/JSON as graphs with the Laufbursche Edition Analysis Tool (leat).
